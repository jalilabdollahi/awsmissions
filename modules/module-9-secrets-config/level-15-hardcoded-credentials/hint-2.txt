Lambda hands the function temporary credentials for its execution role automatically; embedding a user's long-lived key overrides them and leaks the key to anyone who can read the configuration. The cure has three parts: give the function a proper role (trusting lambda.amazonaws.com, with a policy that allows secretsmanager:GetSecretValue on that one secret's ARN and nothing else), switch the function to it and strip the keys, and kill the exposed key, because removing it from the function does not un-leak it. Note that update-function-configuration replaces the whole set of environment variables.
