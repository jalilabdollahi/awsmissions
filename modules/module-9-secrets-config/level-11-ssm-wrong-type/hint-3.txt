Read the current value, then overwrite the parameter with a SecureString under the team's key:

  aws ssm get-parameter --name /harrier/payments/api-key --query Parameter.Value --output text
  aws ssm put-parameter --name /harrier/payments/api-key --type SecureString --key-id alias/harrier-config --value <the value> --overwrite

Do not touch /harrier/payments/timeout-ms.
