An alias is a movable pointer. Here alias/orders-prod and alias/orders-staging both point at the staging key, so everything written through "prod" is encrypted under the wrong key. You do not need to create a new alias or a new key: identify the production key (its tags and description say so; its ciphertext is what the old production record in /orders/prod/sample-record was made with) and move the existing alias onto it. There is a dedicated command for re-pointing an alias.
