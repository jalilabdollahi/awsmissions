Reproduce what the vault sees, then look at what aliases the account really has:

  aws kms encrypt --key-id alias/lanternfish-vault --plaintext aGVsbG8=
  aws kms list-aliases --query 'Aliases[?starts_with(AliasName, `alias/lanternfish`)]'

(Ignore the alias/aws/... entries; those belong to AWS-managed keys.)
