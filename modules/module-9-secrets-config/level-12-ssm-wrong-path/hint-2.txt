Parameter Store names are literal strings: the service reads /lattice/production/..., the data lives under /lattice/prod/..., and there is no alias or fallback between them. The IAM policy of lattice-svc is scoped to the production subtree on purpose, and other teams depend on /lattice/prod, so neither widening the policy nor deleting the old parameters is the answer. The data needs to exist at the names the service expects, with the same types: remember the password is a SecureString.
