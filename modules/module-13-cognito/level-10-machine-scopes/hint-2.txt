A scope has to pass two checks: it must be defined on the resource server, and the client must be allowed to request it. The wording of invalid_scope tells you which check failed ("is invalid" versus "is not allowed for this client"). Expect to fix both, one after the other.

Both update calls replace what they are given instead of adding to it, so repeat everything that should stay (the existing scopes, the grant type, the identity provider).
