Treat it as a live incident: contain, recover, eradicate. Start by looking at what the identity has and what exists that should not:

  aws ssm get-parameter --name /cinder/forensics/leaked-key --with-decryption
  aws iam list-access-keys --user-name cg-build-bot
  aws iam get-user-policy --user-name cg-build-bot --policy-name ci-everything
  aws iam list-users
  aws iam list-roles --query 'Roles[].[RoleName,AssumeRolePolicyDocument.Statement[0].Principal]'

Look for users and roles you cannot account for, especially ones with administrator policies.
