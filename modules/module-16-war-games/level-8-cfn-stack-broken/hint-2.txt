Four problems, all rooted in the template you have in your workspace:

- an Output exports a name that another stack already exports (export names are unique per region), which is why the stack failed
- the table has no protection against being deleted along with the stack, which makes the "delete and recreate" shortcut dangerous
- the function's environment names a table that does not exist
- the function's role has no permission to read the table

Prefer updating the existing stack in place over deleting it.
