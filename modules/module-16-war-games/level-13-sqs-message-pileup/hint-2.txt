Four things stand between a poison message and an alert:

- the redrive policy sends failed messages to a queue name that does not exist
- the receive limit before parking is absurdly high
- the visibility timeout gives a worker far less time than a parcel needs
- no alarm watches the dead-letter queue, and nothing notifies ops

Queue attributes such as RedrivePolicy are set with one call that takes all of them as a JSON map; the redrive policy is itself a JSON string inside that map.
