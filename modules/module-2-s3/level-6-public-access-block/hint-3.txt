Turn off BlockPublicPolicy and RestrictPublicBuckets (leave the two ACL settings true), scope the policy Resource to .../public/*, then apply it:

  aws s3api put-public-access-block --bucket lumen-brand-assets --public-access-block-configuration BlockPublicAcls=true,IgnorePublicAcls=true,BlockPublicPolicy=false,RestrictPublicBuckets=false
  aws s3api put-bucket-policy --bucket lumen-brand-assets --policy file://public-read-policy.json
