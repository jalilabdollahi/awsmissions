Enable versioning, then copy the good version (the newest non-null one) over the current object:

  aws s3api put-bucket-versioning --bucket pixelcart-feature-flags --versioning-configuration Status=Enabled
  aws s3api copy-object --bucket pixelcart-feature-flags --key flags.json --copy-source "pixelcart-feature-flags/flags.json?versionId=<id>"
