Fields that should be evaluated need a key ending in ".$": for example "fullName": {"S.$": "$.fullName"} is right, while "email": {"S": "$.email"} is not. Fix both email and welcomeLine in the same way, then:

  aws stepfunctions update-state-machine --state-machine-arn arn:aws:states:us-east-1:000000000000:stateMachine:saffron-signup --definition file://signup.asl.json
