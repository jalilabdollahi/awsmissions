There are four problems and each one hides the next:
- the rule's event pattern versus what the shop really sends (compare the pattern with your test event),
- which state machine the rule target points at,
- what the target hands to the workflow (an EventBridge target passes the whole event envelope by default; the workflow reads orderId and items at the top level),
- what the workflow's own execution role may invoke (read the failed execution's history).

Fix one, send another test event, read the new symptom. The workspace holds copies of the pattern, the target list and the role policy (type `edit`).
