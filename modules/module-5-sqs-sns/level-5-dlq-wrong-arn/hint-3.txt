Get the real DLQ ARN, put it into the deadLetterTargetArn field of redrive.json, and apply the file to mailroom-outbound:

  aws sqs get-queue-attributes --queue-url <url of mailroom-outbound-dlq> --attribute-names QueueArn
  aws sqs set-queue-attributes --queue-url <url of mailroom-outbound> --attributes file://redrive.json
