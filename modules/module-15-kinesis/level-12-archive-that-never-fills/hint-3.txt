Delete the delivery stream, wait until it is gone, then create it again with the stream as source (archive.json in your workspace has the S3 destination):

  aws firehose delete-delivery-stream --delivery-stream-name pageview-archive
  aws firehose create-delivery-stream --delivery-stream-name pageview-archive --delivery-stream-type KinesisStreamAsSource --kinesis-stream-source-configuration "KinesisStreamARN=<pageviews ARN>,RoleARN=<role ARN>" --extended-s3-destination-configuration file://archive.json
