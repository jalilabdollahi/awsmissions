Repoint the group, then roll the fleet:

  aws autoscaling update-auto-scaling-group --auto-scaling-group-name parcelway-asg --launch-template 'LaunchTemplateName=parcelway-lt,Version=$Latest'
  aws autoscaling start-instance-refresh --auto-scaling-group-name parcelway-asg --preferences MinHealthyPercentage=50

(Use single quotes so your shell does not eat the $.) Follow progress with describe-instance-refreshes, and use bash probe.sh to see the release.
