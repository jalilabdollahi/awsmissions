Replace the rule's conditions with both hostnames spelled correctly. modify-rule overwrites the whole list, so send both values:

  aws elbv2 modify-rule --rule-arn <rule-arn> --conditions '[{"Field":"host-header","Values":["portal.helixpay.example","app.helixpay.example"]}]'

Do not switch to a catch-all pattern like "*": other hosts must still reach the marketing site.
