The worker can list the bucket but not write to it, so authentication and the trust policy are working. Read the role's inline policy:

  aws iam list-role-policies --role-name pixelforge-uploader
  aws iam get-role-policy --role-name pixelforge-uploader --policy-name uploader-access

Look at every field of the statement that covers s3:PutObject, not just Action and Resource.
