A permissions boundary is a ceiling: the role can only do what its policies allow AND its boundary allows. Read the boundary policy (aws iam get-policy / get-policy-version) and compare it with what the function needs. Removing the boundary would work for SSM but also activate the unwanted dynamodb:* grant, so extend the boundary instead. Managed policies are changed by creating a new default version; `edit boundary.json` has the current document.
