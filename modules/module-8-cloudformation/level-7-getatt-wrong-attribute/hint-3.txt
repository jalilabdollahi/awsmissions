For AWS::S3::Bucket the ARN attribute is "Arn", not "BucketArn". Change !GetAtt ArchiveBucket.BucketArn to !GetAtt ArchiveBucket.Arn in template.yaml, then roll the release out again (the stack is updatable after a rolled-back update):

  aws cloudformation update-stack --stack-name orchard-reporting --template-body file://template.yaml --capabilities CAPABILITY_NAMED_IAM
  aws cloudformation wait stack-update-complete --stack-name orchard-reporting
