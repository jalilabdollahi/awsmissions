Export names are unique per account and region; they form a global namespace that other stacks bind to by name. quayside-assets-bucket is already taken by quayside-network, and quayside-web depends on it, so that name must keep pointing at the shared assets bucket. The reporting template was copied from the network stack, including the Export name that should have been changed.

The fix is in the new template: give its export a distinct name. Also remember a stack whose creation failed cannot be reused as is.
